# Angry Judge: The Short Rant Contest

A single-sentence rant about the pettiest of everyday battles: twenty minutes spent fighting the skin-tight plastic seal on a store-bought jar of pickles, ending with a bleeding fingernail, an intact seal, and the creeping suspicion that the jar is mocking you by slowly rotating in your grip. `main.ts` writes the rant, byte-exact, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
